- The completion item is granted only after all seven tier claims and cosmetics are present.

- Users who already completed the v0.21 pass receive only the missing final title on their next claim.
- Existing v0.20 cosmetic backfill remains compatible.

### Privacy and compatibility

- The final title uses the existing seasonal cosmetic inventory and stores no new personal-data category.
- Topics, match IDs, arguments, transcripts, verdicts and judge scores are never copied.
- The reward does not modify Elo, matchmaking, judging or match outcomes.
- Existing v0.21 tier cosmetics, v0.20 season-pass claims and all earlier PvP data remain compatible.
