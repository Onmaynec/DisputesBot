### Added

- Seven exclusive season-pass cosmetics: four badges and three titles.
- `/pass_collection` with private ownership, unlock and equipped-state tracking.
- Token and cosmetic rewards displayed together in `/season_pass`.
- Automatic first-item equip when the matching badge or title slot is empty.
- Audit fields for the exact cosmetic item ID and grant timestamp.
- Alembic migration `0012_season_pass_cosmetics`.

### Reliability

- Tokens, claim audit, cosmetic inventory and loadout updates share one transaction.
- Existing v0.20 tier claims can backfill their missing cosmetic without paying tokens again.
- A pass item already present in inventory is not duplicated; the audit row is repaired instead.
- Each tier maps to one deterministic and unique cosmetic item ID.
- Pass cosmetics are omitted from the ordinary shop and cannot be purchased through the legacy `/buy` balance path.
- Repeated claims remain idempotent after both token and cosmetic rewards are recorded.
- Later season-point growth grants only newly unlocked tiers and their items.

### Privacy and compatibility

- Cosmetic claim audit stores only user ID, season, tier ID, item ID and timestamps.
- Topics, match IDs, arguments, transcripts, verdicts and judge scores are never copied.
- Cosmetics are seasonal, use the existing inventory/loadout tables and support `/equip` and PvP profiles.
- Rewards do not modify season points, Elo, matchmaking, judging or match outcomes.
- Existing v0.20 season pass, v0.19 goal rewards and all earlier PvP data remain compatible.
- `/delete_me` removes claim audit and cosmetic inventory through explicit deletion and `ON DELETE CASCADE`.
